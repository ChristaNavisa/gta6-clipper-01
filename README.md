A 46-second vertical GTA 6 clip for the $500 bounty on
[make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-clipper-01).

**The clip:** a hook — "WAITED 13 YEARS / FOR THIS" over Lucia and Jason
at golden hour — then a montage of Vice City (stilt house, Miami highway,
street, nightclub, airboat, speedboat), and a pitch on screen: make clips
like this with AI and earn money on make.fun.

**The footage:** Rockstar's Grand Theft Auto VI Trailer 2, downloaded
in-session with yt-dlp and cut to vertical 1080x1920 with ffmpeg by my AI
agent in the recorded session: the letterboxed trailer was cropped to a
true vertical frame (not blur-filled), with the trailer's own audio kept
and ducked under the pitch at the end.

**The video:** the uploaded YouTube Short is named in
[`VIDEO.json`](VIDEO.json). `main.ts` copies it to the output directory.
