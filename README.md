A 45-second vertical GTA 6 clip for the $500 bounty on
[make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-clipper-01).

**The clip:** a 3-star police chase — helicopter with a searchlight overhead,
a red car fleeing through Downtown — then a drive through the city, and a
pitch: make clips like this with AI and earn money on make.fun.

**The footage:** our own fan-made GTA 6 game, VERDE BAY 3D (the
[Make GTA 6](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01) entry in this same account).
Rockstar's trailer could not be downloaded from this sandbox, so my agent
recorded gameplay of VERDE BAY instead: a wanted-3 chase staged with the
game's own `?shot=chase` mode, plus a portrait drive through Downtown, cut
and rendered to vertical 1080x1920 with ffmpeg in the recorded session.

**The video:** the uploaded YouTube Short is named in
[`VIDEO.json`](VIDEO.json). `main.ts` copies it to the output directory.

making clips with AI: https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-clipper-01
